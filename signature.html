How to add it in Gmail

  1. Click "Copy signature" above.
  2. Go to Gmail → gear icon top right → "See all settings".
  3. Scroll to the "Signature" section and click "Create new", give it a name (e.g. "Castor").
  4. Click into the empty text box and paste with Ctrl/Cmd+V.
  5. Set this signature as default for new emails and for replies/forwards.
  6. Scroll down and click "Save Changes".
