How to add both in Gmail

  1. Click "Copy signature" under "For new emails" above.
  2. Go to Gmail → gear icon top right → "See all settings".
  3. Scroll to "Signature", click "Create new", name it e.g. "Castor – New", and paste it in.
  4. Now copy the "For replies & forwards" version above, and create a second signature named e.g. "Castor – Reply".
  5. Further down, under "Signature defaults", set "FOR NEW EMAILS USE" to your first signature, and "ON REPLY/FORWARD USE" to the second.
  6. Scroll down and click "Save Changes".
